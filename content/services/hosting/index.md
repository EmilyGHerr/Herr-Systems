## Hosting

Keep your website online, maintained, and in good hands. We help with the technical housekeeping so you can focus on your customers instead of your hosting dashboard.

- Hosting setup, domain connections, and HTTPS
- Backups, routine maintenance, and update planning
- Help with migrations and a real person for support

Businesses that want reliable hosting without managing it themselves.

## Example scope: move a small business website

A hosting project can begin with a simple inventory: the website, domain, current host, connected email, and the person who owns each account. The goal is to move or maintain the website without treating the business's other tools as an afterthought.

This is an example scope. Provider fees, backup retention, update responsibilities, support hours, and any monitoring would be agreed for the actual site; this page is not an uptime or recovery guarantee.

## A practical setup process

1. **Inventory what exists.** Confirm ownership, hosting requirements, domain records, and dependencies before changing anything.
2. **Prepare the destination.** Configure the agreed environment, HTTPS, and a safe way to review the site before changing the public domain.
3. **Plan the switch.** Check forms, redirects, image paths, and any DNS records that must remain in place for email or other services.
4. **Document the ongoing work.** Record access ownership, the update process, backup arrangements, and how to request help.

## Questions worth answering

- What is covered by the hosting provider, and what needs separate maintenance?
- What is backed up, where is it stored, and how would a restore be tested?
- Who owns the domain and hosting account?
- Which changes require additional scope or a separate provider charge?

## A useful handoff

The handoff can include a short account inventory, routine maintenance checklist, and an agreed escalation path. You should understand where the site lives and who is responsible for its moving parts.
