## How much should hosting cost?

Whether you're local to Union, MO or are anywhere else in the United States, finding a spot for your website or app to run on can be a whole ordeal if you don't do this sort of thing all the time.

If you run a small website for your business as an online advertisement to show up in search results and to make your customers comfortable: if you're paying more than $10 a month it's likely that you're overpaying.

If you have an online store, that cost could be more. If you have an application that reads and writes a lot of data, it could be $100+.

It really just kinda depends on what you're doing.
