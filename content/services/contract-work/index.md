## Contract Work

Bring in focused design and development support for a defined project or a stretch of busy weeks. We work with your existing tools, communicate clearly, and hand over work your team can maintain.

- Scoped design and development support
- Collaboration within your existing workflow
- Documented delivery and a practical handoff

Agencies and teams with a project backlog, a delivery deadline, or a temporary skills gap.

## Example scope: support a defined delivery milestone

Contract support can add focused design or development capacity to an existing team. For example, an agency might need an approved page template implemented, a backlog of interface fixes completed, or a feature taken through review and handoff.

This is an illustrative scope, not a statement of current staffing capacity or availability. Responsibilities, working hours, access, communication, and acceptance criteria would be agreed before work starts.

## Fit into the existing workflow

- Use the team's agreed repository, issue tracker, review process, and communication channel.
- Define what is included, what is explicitly excluded, and who approves changes.
- Use appropriate access that can be revoked at the end of the engagement.
- Raise dependencies and scope changes before they become surprises.

## A straightforward delivery process

1. **Agree on the milestone.** Turn the request into concrete deliverables and a shared definition of done.
2. **Establish the working setup.** Confirm the tools, permissions, relevant documentation, and a safe development environment.
3. **Deliver in reviewable pieces.** Make progress visible and test the work against the agreed requirements.
4. **Close with a useful handoff.** Provide implementation notes, test results, known limitations, and any remaining tasks.

## What helps us scope it

Share the kind of support needed, the existing technology, the deadline, and who will review the work. Sample screenshots or a non-sensitive issue summary are enough for an initial conversation; account credentials are not needed.
