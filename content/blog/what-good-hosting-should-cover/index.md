{{< article-section id="know-the-scope" >}}

## Know what is included

“Hosting” can mean anything from server space to a managed service. Ask who handles software updates, certificate renewals, monitoring, and support—and what falls outside the agreement.

The right scope depends on the site. A static brochure site and an online store have different operational needs. Get those responsibilities written down before you need help.

{{< /article-section >}}

{{< article-section id="ask-about-recovery" >}}

## Ask about recovery, not just backups

A backup is only useful if it can be restored. Ask how often copies are made, how long they are kept, where they live, and who handles a recovery.

Discuss what happens if the site breaks after an update or important content is accidentally removed. Recovery expectations should be clear before an incident, not worked out during one.

{{< /article-section >}}

{{< article-section id="keep-your-keys" >}}

## Keep ownership clear

Know who owns your domain, where the site files live, and how access can be transferred. A dependable setup should not depend on one person remembering a password.

Keep a simple record of providers, responsibilities, renewal dates, and support contacts. Good hosting is partly technology and partly making sure nothing important is a mystery.

{{< /article-section >}}
